## Rules

| Rule | Filter | Action |
|---|---|---|
| md-cool | `marketdata/v1/`, blockBlob, tags `age_band=cool` AND `compacted=true` | Cool, 0 days since modification |
| md-cold | `marketdata/v1/`, blockBlob, tags `age_band=cold` AND `compacted=true` | Cold, 0 days (never Archive) |
| raw | `raw/` | Cool @ 30 d, Archive @ 180 d |
| backtests | `backtests/` | Cool @ 30 d, delete @ 365 d |
| exports | `exports/` | delete @ 14 d |
| bench | `bench/` | delete @ 30 d |

`age_band=hot` has no rule: hot data stays in Hot. Tag filters are AND-ed, support only `==`, and never match an untagged
blob, so the derived `_manifest.json` (not tagged by `retag`) can never be tiered. Market data never reaches Archive
(ADR-0004). `raw/` does: replays of `raw/` data older than 180 days need rehydration (hours of latency plus the Archive
retrieval and read meters); acceptable for replay/audit only. SPEC-03 section 4 carries the `compacted=true` guard.

## Assumptions

- Scales (config-contract): S 3 instruments x 5 y, M 10 x 10 y, L 10 x 20 y; bytes as T-121 (10 B/row): 0.5 / 3 / 15 GB.
- **Age convention:** data years have ages 1..years (the newest complete year is 1 year old); band = `retag.age_band`
  (hot < 3 y, cool 3..10 y, cold > 10 y); bytes and parts spread evenly over the data years. T-121's 15/35/50 split is
  the 20-year case only; S and M have no Cold data.
- Parts (storage-layout section 2): monthly for `ticks`, `bars_1s`, `bars_1m`, `bars_1h`; yearly for `bars_1d`; one part
  per partition after compaction. Bar datasets add parts and tags but negligible bytes (bytes are T-121's tick bytes).
- Index tags are billed on every tagged part in every tier, hot included. Cold data write has no meter in `prices.yaml`
  (unpriced). Hot read ops on untagged/hot parts are ignored. The one-off tier move is not amortised in the nets.

Net = at-rest saving - tag cost - (compaction reads + crossing ops) / 12. Plainly: with five tags on all five datasets,
tag-driven tiering is a **net loss at S, M and L**; at L the tag charge alone is 112 % of the at-rest saving, before
compaction reads. The earlier 0.50 EUR one-off sensitivity (45,000 parts, an unsupported count) is 0.51 EUR with T-121's
15/35/50 split and 0.53 EUR with L's age_band split; with the derived 9,800 parts the L one-off is 0.13 EUR. The
Cool->Cold crossing exists only at L (nothing is older than 10 years at S and M). `compact_partition` reads every part of
every partition, including Cool/Cold parts; skipping single already-compacted parts (T-205) would remove most of the
compaction read cost.

## Early-deletion interaction

Early deletion is billed on the time since the move into the tier. Cold has an explicit meter (`blob_cold_early_delete_gb`;
the unit is per GB-month of unmet retention at 0.004 EUR, 90-day minimum: at most 0.0120 EUR/GB). Cool has no early-delete
meter in the API; the Cool fee is **derived** from `blob_cool_stored_gb_month` x remaining days / 30 (at most 0.0088
EUR/GB). Without the `compacted=true` guard the worst case is every part tiered then deleted by compaction. With the guard
only a late restatement into an already tiered partition can trigger re-compaction of a Cool/Cold file and pay a fee
for that one file; accepted. Cool -> Cold happens a year or more after the Cool move, so no Cool fee applies there.

## Proposal (basis for ADR-0015; `retag.py` unchanged)

Tag only `ticks` parts (plus `bars_1s` if its average part size clears break-even), with two tags (`age_band`,
`compacted`). Bar parts stay untagged and therefore stay in Hot (no lifecycle rule matches them). The `dataset`,
`instrument` and `data_year` tags are recoverable from the path (`layout.parse_md_path`).

**Decision rule.** Tag a dataset iff its average part size exceeds the break-even size, at which tag storage plus yearly
compaction read ops equal the tiering saving (hot-band parts pay tags and save nothing):

| Break-even average part size (MB) | S | M | L |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 tags per part | 3.74 | 2.81 | 2.05 |
| 2 tags per part | 1.50 | 1.13 | 0.85 |
| average ticks part (MB) | 2.78 | 2.50 | 6.25 |
| average part, all five datasets (MB) | 0.68 | 0.61 | 1.53 |

Ticks (2.5 to 6.3 MB) clear the 2-tag break-even at every scale, but not the 5-tag one at S and M. The average part over
all five datasets is below the 2-tag break-even at S and M, which is why bars should stay untagged. `bars_1s` part size is
not modelled (T-121 has tick bytes only); apply the rule to a measured size (T-503).

**Ticks only tagged (bars untagged), net EUR/month:**

| | S | M | L |
|---|---|---|---|
| tagged parts | 180 | 1200 | 2400 |
| tag cost at 5 tags | 0.0031 | 0.0206 | 0.0412 |
| tag cost at 2 tags | 0.0012 | 0.0082 | 0.0165 |
| one-off total EUR | 0.0016 | 0.0137 | 0.0407 |
| compaction reads EUR/year | 0.0027 | 0.0220 | 0.2622 |
| net at 5 tags | -0.0008 | -0.0023 | +0.0861 |
| **net at 2 tags** | **+0.0010** | **+0.0100** | **+0.1108** |

So the Proposal is net-positive at all three scales (S about break-even); the current design is negative at all three.
