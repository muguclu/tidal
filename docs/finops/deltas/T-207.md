# Cost delta T-207 (storage lifecycle policy, `modules/storage/lifecycle.tf`)

Status: MODELLED. Unit prices from `docs/finops/prices.yaml` (captured 2026-10-21, EUR, westeurope). No measurement yet;
T-503 replaces these with Cost Management numbers. The lifecycle policy itself has no charge; the cost is the tier-change
transactions and the data-write/early-deletion meters below.

## Rules

| Rule | Filter | Action |
|---|---|---|
| md-cool | `marketdata/v1/`, blockBlob, tag `age_band=cool` AND `compacted=true` | Cool, 0 days since modification |
| md-cold | `marketdata/v1/`, blockBlob, tag `age_band=cold` AND `compacted=true` | Cold, 0 days |
| raw | `raw/` | Cool @ 30 d, Archive @ 180 d |
| backtests | `backtests/` | Cool @ 30 d, delete @ 365 d |
| exports | `exports/` | delete @ 14 d |
| bench | `bench/` | delete @ 30 d |

`age_band=hot` has no rule: hot data stays in Hot. Blob index tag filters are AND-ed, support only `==`, and never match an
untagged blob, so the derived `_manifest.json` (not tagged by `retag`) can never be tiered. Market data never reaches
Archive (ADR-0004); `raw/` does (rehydration takes hours and costs the Archive retrieval + read meters, acceptable for
verbatim source responses used for replay/audit only).

## Deviation from SPEC-03 section 4: `compacted=true` guard (needs sign-off)

The spec table has `age_band` as the only marketdata filter. Compaction runs yearly after retag and deletes the old parts
of a partition after writing the merged one. A part tiered to Cool/Cold before that is billed the early-deletion fee for
the unmet minimum (Cool 30 d, Cold 90 d) plus a tier-change write, on bytes that are about to be deleted. Requiring
`compacted=true` tiers only parts that survive compaction. `compact_partition` tags every kept part `compacted=true`,
including partitions that already had one part, so each old partition becomes eligible after one compaction run; restated
parts (compacted=false) stay Hot until the next yearly run. Cost of the guard: a partition waits for its first compaction
before it leaves Hot, at most one year of Hot rate on the not-yet-compacted bytes (Hot minus Cool = 0.0084 EUR/GB-month,
Hot minus Cold = 0.0132 EUR/GB-month). Cost of no guard in the worst case (every part tiered, then deleted within days):
Cool 0.0088 EUR/GB (30 d of Cool) or Cold 0.004 x 3 = 0.012 EUR/GB (90 d of Cold early delete) plus 0.0022 EUR/GB Cool
data write, per deleted byte. Both are small; the guard is chosen because it is free to run and makes the interaction
deterministic. A re-compaction of an already tiered part (late restatement into an old partition) can still pay an
early-deletion fee for that one file; accepted.

Cool to Cold moves (band `cool` to `cold`, once per partition per decade) happen about a year after the Cool move, so no
Cool early-deletion fee applies.

## Per-GB effect (marketdata)

| Tier | At rest EUR/GB-month | Saving vs Hot EUR/GB-month | One-off move into tier (data write EUR/GB) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hot | 0.0172 | - | - |
| Cool | 0.0088 | 0.0084 (49 %) | 0.0022 |
| Cold | 0.0040 | 0.0132 (77 %) | not in prices.yaml; 10K writes 0.1584 only |

Reads: retrieval per GB read is 0.0088 EUR (Cool) and 0.0264 EUR (Cold); read ops 0.0088 / 0.088 EUR per 10K. Backtests
that scan old years pay these, so the saving above shrinks if cold bands are read often (T-503 measures).

## Transactions

- Tag writes (`Set Blob Tags`, yearly retag + compact): billed as "other" operations, 0.0038 EUR/10K. Assumed 1 tag write
  per part per year plus 1 at creation. Part counts: S ~ 1.5K parts, M ~ 9K, L ~ 45K (about 20 years x instruments x 12
  months, ticks/intraday monthly, daily yearly). Yearly: L = 90K writes = 0.034 EUR per year. Negligible.
- Tier changes: one Cool write op (0.088 EUR/10K) or Cold write op (0.1584 EUR/10K) per part moved, once per decade of age:
  L: 45K x 0.5 (cool+cold share) -> about 0.2 EUR one-off plus Cool data write 0.0022 EUR/GB x ~5 GB (35 % of 15 GB) = 0.011 EUR.
- Lifecycle list/evaluation: not billed separately beyond the above.
