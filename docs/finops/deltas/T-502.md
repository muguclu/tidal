## Trade-offs and levers

- **Reuse of the storage account vs a dedicated one:** reuse adds no fixed cost and no extra resource; the price is that the
  Cost Management write identity and the reader sit next to market data (mitigated by container-scoped roles). A dedicated
  account would be needed if Cost Management cannot write to a shared-key-disabled account (NEEDS-CLOUD, see module README).
- **Resource-group scope vs subscription scope:** no subscription rights are needed, but the AKS node group `MC_*`, the
  largest cost item (C4), is not in the export. A subscription-scope export would need subscription Owner/Contributor to apply.
- **Daily month-to-date vs hourly detail:** the export is daily; C10 is evidenced per day, not per hour.
- **Operational effort:** one more scheduled workflow, read-only; it is skipped until the repo variables exist.
- No lifecycle rule on `cost-exports` yet: 12 months cost less than EUR 0.0003, so deletion is not worth a rule.
