## Cases (EUR/month)

| Case | Compute | Storage (32 GB, upper bound) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paused all month (auto-pause, no compute billed) | 0 | 3.85 | **3.85** |

| 40 h active at 2 vCore (ceiling) | 40.40 | 3.85 | **44.25** |

The 40 h case is bounded by the 0.5 vCore floor and the 2 vCore ceiling; real usage sits between. Storage is
modelled at the full 32 GB max size, an upper bound (billing follows allocated data space).

## Not modelled

- Idle time before auto-pause (up to 60 min after the last connection is billed at the active rate, at least 0.5 vCore).
- Backup storage beyond the included amount, and the log/audit sink (auditing is deferred to T-407).

## Free offer (conditional on `use_free_offer`)

The "free offer" rows above apply only when `use_free_offer = true` (default, dev-only), applied through azapi per
ADR-0011 (azurerm 4.81.0 has no native arguments). With `use_free_offer = false` (prod, or the subscription already
holds 10 free databases) the list-price rows apply: paused 3.85, 40 h active 13.95 to 44.25. The free-offer figures stay
MODELLED until the first apply confirms the API accepts the property as an update on a just-created database.

## SKU justification and levers

- GP serverless: the only tier that auto-pauses; DTU tiers cannot reach zero compute. SKU, min/max vCore,
  auto-pause and 32 GB per SPEC-07 section 2.
- Zone redundancy off and Local backup redundancy: the hot store is re-creatable, durable data is in Blob.
- Levers: `auto_pause_delay_in_minutes`, `min_capacity`, `max_size_gb`; `make park` needs no action because the
  database pauses itself.
