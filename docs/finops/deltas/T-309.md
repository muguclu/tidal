(`python -I docs/finops/deltas/T-309_model.py`). Every figure below is that script's output; no number is typed in.

(`synth-daily` cron, `tiering-yearly` cron Jan 2) and a third, `tiering` (Service Bus event, KEDA), only when `enable_tiering_event_job` is true (default false), four `AcrPull` role assignments (free).

## Notes

- Priced delta: **+11.53 EUR/month** while the App Service plan exists (cost-model row "App Service", which says ~12), 0.00 when parked with `enable_app_service = false`. Container Apps adds 0.00 to the priced total; it stays within the free grants in every scenario above (default flags peak at 74% in January), matching the cost-model row (0-3).
- Log Analytics: the Container Apps environment now ships console and system logs to the shared workspace. Its 0.2 GB/day cap bounds the cost (cost-model row "Log Analytics + App Insights", no change to that row); the ingestion volume of this environment is UNMEASURED until the first C5 export.
- UNPRICED: Container Apps vCPU-seconds and GiB-seconds above the grant (meters `containerapps_vcpu_active_second`, `containerapps_memory_active_gib_second`, rounded to 0.0 by the API), outbound data transfer. Idle-usage meters do not apply to the jobs and to the query app at 0 replicas.
- The `tiering` event job is behind `enable_tiering_event_job` (default false) and runs `tidal-worker --queue tiering-jobs --max-messages 1` (T-303). The KEDA scaler needs Data Owner on `tiering-jobs` to read queue counts; that identity and the flag flip are T-403 (noted in TASKS). With the flag off it costs nothing. The queue now has a 1 day TTL and dead-letters on expiry like `backtest-jobs`.
- `AcrPull` assignments for `id-api` (only while `enable_app_service` is true), `id-query`, `id-ingest`, `id-tiering` are in `envs/dev/apps.tf` and in the SPEC-06 section 2 ACR column. Free.
