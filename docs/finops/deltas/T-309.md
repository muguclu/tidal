## Notes

- Priced delta: **+11.53 EUR/month** while the App Service plan exists (cost-model row "App Service", which says ~12), 0.00 when parked with `enable_app_service = false`. Container Apps adds 0.00 to the priced total; it stays within the free grants in both scenarios, matching the cost-model row (0-3).
- UNPRICED: Container Apps vCPU-seconds and GiB-seconds above the grant (meters `containerapps_vcpu_active_second`, `containerapps_memory_active_gib_second`, rounded to 0.0 by the API), outbound data transfer. Idle-usage meters do not apply to the jobs and to the query app at 0 replicas.
- Operational caveat: the `tiering` event job runs `tidal-tiering run` but nothing completes the Service Bus message until the T-303 consumer exists, so one message keeps re-triggering the job (one execution at a time, 30 minute cap) until its TTL or max delivery count. The grant covers a few of these; the T-303 consumer ends the loop.
- Role assignments for the Service Bus scaler (Data Receiver on `tiering-jobs` for `id-tiering`) and the data roles are T-403. The `AcrPull` assignments for `id-api`, `id-query`, `id-ingest`, `id-tiering` are added here and are missing from the SPEC-06 section 2 table (its ACR column is empty for them).
