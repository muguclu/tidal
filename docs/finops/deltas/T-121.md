# Cost delta T-121 (storage + containers, key_vault modules)

Status: MODELLED. Unit prices from `docs/finops/prices.yaml` (captured 2026-10-21, EUR, westeurope), read with
`tidal.finops.fetch_prices.load_prices`. No measurement yet; T-503 replaces these with Cost Management numbers.

## Volume assumptions (stated, not measured)

- Row counts: S = 50M rows (PLANNING gate, Phase 1), L = up to 1.5B rows (PLANNING section 1). The M scale has no
  row count in any spec; **assumption: M = 500M rows (10x S)**. To be confirmed.
- Size: 10 bytes/row, the top of the 5-10 bytes/tick range in storage-layout section 2 (conservative).
- Data-age split (20 years of uniformly generated history): 15 % under 3 y (`age_band=hot`), 35 % 3-10 y
  (`cool`), 50 % over 10 y (`cold`). The `cold` band is assumed to map to the Cold access tier (ADR-0004: no Archive
  for market data).
- The raw/reference/backtests/exports/bench containers, SQL and transactions are not included (small relative to the above).

## Key Vault (Standard)

Operations 0.0264 EUR per 10K. Assumed 10K to 100K operations per month (secret reads at start-up of a handful of
workloads, no per-request reads): 0.026 to 0.26 EUR/month. No HSM keys, no certificates; no per-secret charge.
